# Linky IP review (2026-10-24)

An engineering review of what the app ships and where intellectual-property risk could come from. It is not legal advice: before a public launch, have a trademark lawyer and a patent attorney confirm the two items marked **Needs a lawyer**.

## Summary

| Area | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Open-source code licenses | Clear. All permissive (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, ISC, 0BSD, BlueOak). No GPL or AGPL anywhere. |
| Fonts | Clear. Inter, Fraunces, Press Start 2P and VT323 are all SIL Open Font License 1.1. |
| Images and art | Clear. Demo avatars, starfield and icons are generated by our own scripts. |
| City data | Clear with attribution. GeoNames, CC BY 4.0. Credit now shown in Settings > Credits and licenses. |
| ML models | Clear. MediaPipe face landmarker (Apache-2.0), TensorFlow.js (Apache-2.0), NSFWJS model (MIT). |
| Personality quiz and type names | Clear. Original questions, original nicknames (not the 16Personalities ones), MBTI® disclaimer shown. |
| Love-language quiz | Low risk after fix. Original questions; disclaimer added for The 5 Love Languages®. |
| Profile prompts | Fixed. Several prompts matched Hinge's wording; all 20 were rewritten in our own words. |
| App name "Linky" | **Needs a lawyer.** "Linky" is a well-known registered mark of Enedis (French smart meter), and an app called "Linky Real" is on the App Store. |

## Details

### Code dependencies
Checked with `pnpm licenses list` for production and development dependencies. Production: MIT (71 packages), Apache-2.0 (60, mostly Firebase), BSD-3-Clause (13), ISC (5), BSD-2-Clause, 0BSD, Apache-2.0 AND MIT (tfjs-layers). Development only: also MPL-2.0 (lightningcss, file-level copyleft, not shipped as source changes) and CC-BY-4.0 (caniuse-lite, cities.json data). Apache-2.0 and MIT need their notices kept when distributing; the bundled JS keeps license comments, and the source repo carries them.

### Fonts
All four families come from Google Fonts under OFL 1.1, which allows commercial use and embedding. No action needed beyond the credit line.

### Images
- Demo avatars and photo scenes: SVG generated by `scripts/gen-seed-sql.ts` / `shared/content`.
- Space map starfield: generated pixel art (`apps/pwa/public/space/stars.png`).
- App icon: our own SVG.
No stock photos or third-party art are shipped.

### Data
`apps/pwa/src/data/cities.json` is derived from GeoNames via the `all-the-cities` (MIT) and `cities.json` (CC BY 4.0) packages. CC BY requires attribution to end users who receive the data; the app now shows it under Settings > Credits and licenses, and the README credits it too.

### Personality
16 type codes (INTJ and so on) describe Jungian preferences and are widely used; the trademarks are "Myers-Briggs", "MBTI" and "Myers-Briggs Type Indicator", which we only use in the disclaimer. The quiz questions and the type nicknames ("The Strategist", "The Spark"...) are original. Do not switch to the 16Personalities names ("Architect", "Protagonist"...), which are theirs.

### Love languages
"The 5 Love Languages" is a registered trademark. We use "love languages" descriptively and the five category names, as Bumble's love-language badges do, with an original quiz. A disclaimer is now shown in the quiz and in Settings. Avoid "5 Love Languages" in the app name, store listing or marketing.

### Prompts
Short phrases are generally not protected by copyright, but copying a competitor's curated list wording-for-wording invites a dispute. All 20 prompts were rewritten (for example "Two truths and a lie" became "Guess which one is false", "A perfect Sunday looks like" became "My ideal lazy day includes"). Demo profiles and the Supabase seed were regenerated to match.

### Name
"Linky" is a famous Enedis trademark in France and the EU (the Linky smart electricity meter), and "Linky Real" is an existing App Store app. Different goods matter in trademark law, but a famous mark gets wider protection, and Quebec and France are likely markets. Recommendation: run a clearance search (CIPO, USPTO, EUIPO, INPI) in class 9 (software) and class 45 (dating services) before spending on branding, and keep a fallback name ready.

### Patents
- Swiping: Match Group sued Bumble over Tinder's swipe patents; the case settled in 2020. Linky does not use swiping.
- Liking or commenting on a specific photo or prompt: Hinge's core mechanic. A quick search found no patent on it, but Match Group holds many dating patents.
- Space map: showing people as faces orbiting at a distance set by their match score is our own design; no matching patent was found in a quick search.
Recommendation: a freedom-to-operate search by a patent attorney before a public launch, focused on Match Group patents.

## Changes made in this review
1. Rewrote all 20 profile prompts; regenerated demo profiles and `supabase/seed.sql`.
2. Added The 5 Love Languages® disclaimer to the love-language quiz.
3. Added Settings > Credits and licenses (GeoNames CC BY 4.0, OFL fonts, MediaPipe, TensorFlow.js, NSFWJS, MBTI® and 5 Love Languages® disclaimers).
4. Removed "Hinge-style" from the README's description.
